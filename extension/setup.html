Nudge for Chrome

Paste your app link (Config tab of your Google Sheet). It stays on this computer.

What it does

  • Click the Nudge icon (or press Ctrl+Shift+L) to open the full Nudge website. Right-click the icon for the side panel, Work or Booth.
  • Select text on any page, right-click, then Nudge: add as a deadline. Include a time ("by 3pm") or pick one after.
  • Select a task, right-click, then Nudge: time-block. Add a time and length ("10-11:30", "45m") or it goes in your next free slot.
  • The icon shows how many deadlines are open. Red means one is late.
  • Late deadlines also nag you here on the desktop, with Done and +30 min buttons.
